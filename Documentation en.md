# **FINAL PROJECT DATA STRUCTURES / PROGRAMMING LOGIC II**

# **Develop the following program:**

A hotel chain wants to keep track of the guests it receives at the hotel. For this purpose, it requires a program that allows it to:

Store the following information in linked lists:

1. Guests (ID document, name, age, gender)

2. Rooms in which guests will stay (room number, type (single, double, family), and status (available, occupied)).

3. Hotel services that can be purchased by guests (code, service name, price).

When a guest arrives at the hotel, a check-in record must be stored in a linked list. Each registered guest record must contain their personal data and the assigned room. Keep in mind that if a guest purchases a service at any time, it must be logged in the guest's record. Additionally, there must be a service queue where arriving guests are placed to wait for service and subsequent registration. Once a guest is registered, they must be removed from the service queue.

Similarly, when the guest checks out of the hotel (check-out), the total amount to be paid for their stay must be displayed taking into account the following:

The room rate per night depends on the room type:

- Single: $155,000

- Double: $80,000

- Family: $55,000

Keep in mind that the services offered by the hotel are:

1. Restaurant, which has standard meal prices: Breakfast $10,000, Lunch $12,000, and Dinner $14,000.

2. Laundry service: $2,000 per garment.

3. Massage service: $25,000 per person.

4. Safe deposit box service: $15,000.

5. Vehicle rental: $30,000 per day.

Keep in mind that the massage service has a capacity limit: only one person can be served at a time, and only 5 people can wait in line in the service area. These waiting guests must be stored in a service queue. Once a guest has been served, they must be removed from the queue so another person can enter to use the service.

The hotel applies discounts according to the following table:

| **Age** <br> **Discount** |
|---|
| Under 5 years old <br> 15% |
| Over 60 years old <br> 25% |
| All other ages <br> 5% |

Payments can be made using the following methods:

- Cash

- Debit card

- Credit card

An array must store the count of guests who paid using each payment method.

A stack must record the count of guests according to the service purchased.

In addition, the system must display:

- The total number of guests who have stayed at the hotel.

- The total number of guests by gender.

- The total revenue collected from guests according to their gender.

- The overall total revenue collected by the hotel.

- The total revenue collected in the hotel by each payment method.

- The total revenue collected by each service.

- The total amount of discounts granted.

- The total amount generated by each age group established in the table above.

- The guest who paid the most and the guest who paid the least.

- The percentage of guests served within the age ranges mentioned in the table above.

- The percentage of revenue collected from each service offered by the hotel.

- The percentage of guests (in quantity and revenue) according to payment methods.

- The percentage of guests by gender.

- The percentage of money collected by each payment method.

- The total money collected for each age range of staying guests.

- The number of people currently queuing for the massage service.

- The average number of people who have used the massage service.

- The average number of people who have remained in the service queue to be checked in at the hotel.
